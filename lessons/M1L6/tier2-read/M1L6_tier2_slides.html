Short Answer: Where Every Mark Comes From
"Calculate the average total cost of a workshop producing Q = 120 units, with total cost $480. [2 marks]"
Mark-scheme stepWhat the examiner wants to seeMarks
FormulaATC = TC ÷ Q1
Substitution + answer480 ÷ 120 = $4.00 (with unit)1
Common mark lossWriting "4" with no working AND no unit−1
Rule of thumb: a 2-mark calculation almost always awards 1 mark for METHOD and 1 mark for the correct, labelled ANSWER — so even a wrong final number can earn a mark if the method is visible.

IGCSE Levels of Response
Band 1 vs. Band 3 on an 8-Mark (d) Question
"Discuss whether the government should introduce a price ceiling on rent." [8]Band 1 (1–3 marks)Band 3 (7–8 marks)
StructureOne-sided; states an opinion with little developmentTwo-sided; explores BOTH benefits and drawbacks with development
Content"Yes, it helps poor people afford rent." (no mechanism explained)Explains the mechanism (lower legal max price → more affordable) AND the risk (landlords supply fewer rentals → shortage)
JudgementNo final judgement, or a judgement with no supporting reasonA clear judgement ("depends on how far below equilibrium the ceiling is set") supported by the analysis above
