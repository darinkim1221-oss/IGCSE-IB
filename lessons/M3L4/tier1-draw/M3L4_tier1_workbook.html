Warm-up
Show what you discovered today
1. What is the birth rate? Circle the best answer.
  • The number of people moving into a country each year
  • The number of babies born per 1,000 people each year
  • The average age of everyone in a country
2. Fill in the blank: the difference between the number of people moving INTO a country and the number moving OUT is called ______________ ______________.
3. Match each factor to what it most affects.
Quality of healthcare and clean water
Jobs and wages in another country
Access to family planning
Choices: Death rate · Net migration · Birth rate
4. In the Village of 100 People, why did the village's average age rise over time? Explain in 1–2 sentences.
5. In the Candy Economy, 10 candies were shared 6-2-1-1-0 among 5 classmates. Was this an equal share? Explain why or why not.
6. What is the difference between absolute poverty and relative poverty?
7. Name ONE cause of poverty.
  • Unemployment
  • Low wages
  • Illness
  • Age
  • Environmental factors
8. Name ONE government policy that could help reduce poverty, and explain how it helps.
🏠 Family Discussion Mission
Ask a family member: "Do you think everyone in our community has a fair chance to succeed? Why or why not?" Write down their answer.
Answer Key
1. "The number of babies born per 1,000 people each year." — This is the definition of birth rate.
2. Net migration.
3. Healthcare/clean water: Death rate. Jobs/wages abroad: Net migration. Family planning access: Birth rate.
4. Better healthcare meant people lived longer, while fewer babies were being born — together, these two changes pushed the average age of the village up.
5. No — it was NOT an equal share. An equal share of 10 candies among 5 people would be 2 each, but instead one person got 6 (three times the equal share) while another got 0.
6. Absolute poverty means not having enough income to meet basic needs (food, water, shelter) — the same standard everywhere. Relative poverty means having much less income than the average person in your OWN country, even if basic needs are met.
7. Accept any one of: unemployment, low wages, illness, age, or environmental factors.
8. Accept any of the six policies with a correct explanation (e.g. "A national minimum wage — it sets a legal floor on pay so working people earn enough to meet basic needs").
