Economies & Diseconomies of Scale
Drawing REQUIRED at IGCSE (newly explicit 2027–29)
The Average Total Cost (ATC) Diagram
Falling arm (left): economies of scale — average cost falls as output rises, because fixed costs are spread over more units and the firm gains efficiencies.

Q(MES): the minimum efficient scale — the output level where ATC is lowest.

Rising arm (right): diseconomies of scale — average cost rises again past Q(MES), often due to management/coordination problems in an oversized firm.
