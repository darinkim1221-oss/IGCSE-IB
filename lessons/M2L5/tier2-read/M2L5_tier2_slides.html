Hook
Who Really Steers the Economy?
THINK ABOUT IT
A government spends and taxes. A central bank moves interest rates. New rules can change how easy it is to work or invest. All three can be aimed at the SAME problem — but they work on different timescales, and each one creates a trade-off somewhere else. By the end of this lesson, you'll be able to argue which lever fits which problem, and defend the judgement.
