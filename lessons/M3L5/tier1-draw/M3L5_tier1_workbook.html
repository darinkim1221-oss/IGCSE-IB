Warm-up
Show what you discovered today
1. What is the difference between growth and development? Circle the best answer.
  • They mean exactly the same thing
  • Growth is an increase in a country's output; development is a broader improvement in living standards
  • Development is only about how much money a country's government has
2. Fill in the blank: a country's total income divided by its population is called ______________ ______________ ______________.
3. Match each factor to what it most affects.
Investing more in schools and training
Access to good hospitals and clean water
Having valuable oil, gas, or minerals
Choices: Natural resources · Education · Healthcare
4. What is globalisation? Explain in 1–2 sentences.
5. In the Town Hall role play, name ONE voice who was excited about the giant company arriving, AND ONE voice who was worried. Explain why each felt that way.
6. In Country Detective, what THREE clues did you use to guess how developed a country was?
7. What does "sustainability" mean?
  • Using up resources as fast as possible
  • Meeting today's needs without making it harder for future generations to meet theirs
  • Only rich countries need to worry about the environment
8. Name ONE thing that could help the whole world develop without damaging the planet.
🏠 Family Discussion Mission
Ask a family member: "If you could send one thing to help a community with fewer resources than ours, what would it be — and why?" Write down their answer.
Answer Key
1. "Growth is an increase in a country's output; development is a broader improvement in living standards." — Growth is about GDP rising; development includes health, education and income together.
2. GDP per person (accept "GDP per capita").
3. Schools/training: Education. Hospitals/clean water: Healthcare. Oil/gas/minerals: Natural resources.
4. Globalisation means countries, businesses, and cultures around the world becoming more connected — through trade, travel, technology, and communication.
5. Accept any reasonable pairing, e.g.: the Local Worker was excited because the factory could mean steady jobs and income; the Local Shop Owner was worried the giant company might sell things too cheaply to compete with.
6. GDP per person, average years of school, and life expectancy.
7. "Meeting today's needs without making it harder for future generations to meet theirs." — This is the definition of sustainability.
8. Accept any reasonable answer, e.g. investing in cleaner energy, protecting natural resources, or designing growth that doesn't use up resources faster than they can be replaced.
