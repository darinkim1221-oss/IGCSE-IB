Two Rival Indicators
Real GDP per Head vs. HDI
No formal D/S diagram required — this is the key VISUAL
GDP per Capita vs. HDI, Side by Side
Reading the chart: each country has TWO bars — GDP per capita (gold) and HDI ×100 (teal).

When the bars diverge (one much taller than the other), that country's income and its human development are telling two different stories — exactly the gap that makes a single indicator misleading on its own.
