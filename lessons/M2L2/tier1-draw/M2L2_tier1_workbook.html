🏠 Family Discussion Mission
Ask a family member: "Do you think a country with a bigger economy is automatically a better place to live? Why or why not?" Write down their answer and one reason they gave.
Answer Key
1. "The total value of everything a country produces in a year." — GDP is about total OUTPUT, not happiness or debt.
2. Population. — GDP per person spreads the total GDP evenly across everyone in the country.
3. GDP → B. GDP per person → A. HDI → C.
4. Boomtown's high GDP hid that the profit wasn't shared fairly (the boss kept most of it) and that the river got polluted. GDP counts total output, but says nothing about who benefits or the environmental cost.
5. Accept two bars with Boomtown taller than Fairland (e.g. 95 vs 65) — the key skill is drawing/reading a simple bar graph.
6. Accept any answer using a game example correctly, e.g. "No — Country A had very high income but only medium happiness because of long hours and stress, while Country B had middle income but high happiness because of strong community ties."
7. Accept any reasoned answer — check the reason actually matches the choice (e.g. "Low money, high HDI, because I'd rather live a long healthy life with good schools").
8. Accept any reasonable answer: unpaid work (chores, volunteering), how fairly the money is shared, damage to the environment, or people's happiness/well-being. It matters because a country could have a high GDP and still have serious problems GDP doesn't show.
