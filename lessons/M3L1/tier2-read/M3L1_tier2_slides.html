Hook
Can Trade Really Make BOTH Sides Richer?
THINK ABOUT IT
It sounds too good to be true: two countries trade, and somehow BOTH end up with more total output than before, without either one working any harder. Today you'll build the exact numbers behind this claim — and meet the formal model IB uses to explain it, even when one country is better at literally everything.
