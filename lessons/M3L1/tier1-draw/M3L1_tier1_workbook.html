Warm-up
Show what you discovered today
1. What is specialisation (by a country)? Circle the best answer.
  • Making everything a country needs, all by itself
  • Focusing on producing what a country makes best or cheapest
  • Refusing to buy anything from other countries
2. Fill in the blank: trade between countries with no taxes or limits on what crosses the border is called ______________ ______________.
3. In the Trading Card Economy game, match each round to what happened.
No Trade round
Free Trade round
Choices: Both teams end up with more kinds of goods · Each team only has its own 2 kinds of goods
4. Why does Messi pay a gardener instead of mowing his own lawn, even though he could probably do it fast? Explain in 1–2 sentences.
5. Pick ONE item from the Backpack Map and write down where it likely came from and why that country specialises in making it.
6. Name ONE advantage and ONE disadvantage of a country specialising in just a few industries.
7. Name ONE advantage and ONE disadvantage of free trade.
8. Why can trade make TWO countries both end up better off, instead of just one winning? Explain in your own words.
🏠 Family Discussion Mission
Ask a family member: "Can you find something in our home that was made in another country? Where do you think it came from, and why?" Write down what you found.
Answer Key
1. "Focusing on producing what a country makes best or cheapest." — Specialisation is about focus, not self-sufficiency.
2. Free trade. — No taxes or limits on goods crossing the border.
3. No Trade: each team only has its own 2 kinds of goods. Free Trade: both teams end up with more kinds of goods.
4. Even though Messi could mow fast, every hour spent mowing is an hour NOT spent on football or endorsements, which pay him far more — that's his opportunity cost. Paying a gardener frees his time for his highest-value activity.
5. Accept any reasonable answer matching the Backpack Map content (e.g. "T-shirt — Bangladesh, because it specialises in low-cost textile manufacturing").
6. Advantage: more efficient production, better quality, more jobs in that industry. Disadvantage: becoming too dependent on a few industries, which is risky if demand for that product falls. Accept any reasonable matching pair.
7. Advantage: more choice and often lower prices for consumers. Disadvantage: local businesses that can't compete with cheaper imports may struggle or close. Accept any reasonable matching pair.
8. Accept any reasonable explanation touching on: each country focuses on what it makes best/cheapest, then trades for the rest — so both countries end up with access to MORE kinds of goods than if each tried to make everything alone.
