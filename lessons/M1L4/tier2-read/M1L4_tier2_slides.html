Hypothesis
Every time a government intervenes in a market, someone gains — and someone loses — even when the goal is fairness.
TEST IT
If this is true, we should be able to draw exactly what an intervention does to a market — and name, concretely, who is better off and who is worse off. Today: the diagrams examiners expect you to draw cold (price ceiling, price floor, indirect tax, subsidy), and the wider toolkit of market-failure fixes.
