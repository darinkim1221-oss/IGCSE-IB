Short Answer: Where Every Mark Comes From
"A country's current account shows: trade in goods −$40bn, trade in services +$18bn, primary income +$6bn, secondary income −$2bn. Calculate the current account total and state whether it is a deficit or surplus. [3 marks]"
Mark-scheme stepWhat the examiner wants to seeMarks
Method−40 + 18 + 6 − 21
Correct total= −18 $bn1
Correct labelA DEFICIT (negative total)1
Rule of thumb: a multi-step calculation almost always awards separate marks for METHOD, correct ANSWER, and correct LABELLING — so showing every step protects marks even if one step goes wrong.

StructureOne-sided; states an opinion with little developmentTwo-sided; explores BOTH the case for and against with development
Content"Yes, trade helps everyone." (no mechanism, no example)Explains comparative advantage's gains AND names a real-world constraint (e.g. weak infrastructure limiting how much a country can benefit) as evidence
JudgementNo final judgement, or one with no supporting reasonA clear judgement ("benefits are real but uneven, depending on a country's starting infrastructure and institutions") supported by the analysis above
