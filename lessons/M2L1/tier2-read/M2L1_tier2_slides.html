The Labour Market & Minimum Wage
Without a minimum wage: the labour demand and supply curves for workers cross at an equilibrium wage — every firm that wants a worker at that wage finds one.

With a minimum wage above equilibrium: more people want to work at the higher wage (Qs) than firms want to hire (Qd) — the gap is a labour SURPLUS (unemployment).
