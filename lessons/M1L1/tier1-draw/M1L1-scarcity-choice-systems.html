The Big Secret
What is scarcity?
Scarcity = our wants are never-ending, but the resources to meet them (time, money, stuff) are limited. So we always have to choose.
🙋
Needs — things you can't live without (water, food, shelter)
😍
Wants — nice to have, but you'd survive without them (a console, new shoes)
🆓
Free goods — unlimited, no cost (sunlight, air) · Economic goods — limited, must be used wisely
💡 No matter how rich you are, or how big your country is, resources run out. That's why every person and every country has to make choices.
