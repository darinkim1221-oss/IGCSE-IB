🏠 Family Discussion Mission
Ask a family member: "What's one big opportunity cost you've faced this year — with money, time, or your career?" Write down what they chose, and what they gave up to get it.
Answer Key
1. "There aren't enough resources to give everyone everything they want." — Scarcity is about finite resources vs. infinite wants, not just money.
2. Opportunity cost. — The next-best thing you gave up when you chose something else.
3. 1–C (Land → Rent), 2–A (Labour → Wages), 3–D (Capital → Interest), 4–B (Enterprise → Profit).
4. Sample answer: "There are only 15 laptops but 28 students want to use one, so not everyone can — that's finite resources vs. more wants than we can supply." Accept any answer that names the finite resource and the unmet want.
5. F, O, F, O. — A fact can be checked/proven; an opinion is a feeling or judgement.
6. Accept any curve showing that more of one (e.g. gaming) means less of the other (sleep) — the key idea is the trade-off, not a "correct" shape.
7. Accept any reasoned answer — check the reason actually matches the system chosen (e.g. "Market, because shops can react fast" or "Planned, because everyone's needs get covered").
8. Open answer — look for something genuinely limited (time, attention, a favourite teacher's help, weekend hours) with a believable reason.
9. False. — Wants grow too. A richer country still can't have literally everything at once — scarcity just moves to a higher level.
10. Sample: the entrepreneur risks their time/money and might fail; their reward, if it works, is called profit.
